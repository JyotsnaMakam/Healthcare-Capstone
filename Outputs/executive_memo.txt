To: Head of Scheduling Operations
Subject: No-show outreach model and controlled pilot recommendation

I recommend piloting the transparent logistic-regression no-show model as a decision-support tool, with staff review before outreach. On the reproducible stratified 240-record holdout, it achieved 81.2% accuracy at a 0.50 cutoff. Given the stated INR 50 false-alarm cost and INR 400 missed-no-show cost, the cost-derived cutoff is 0.1111; at that cutoff the holdout cost was INR 6,550. For comparison, the logistic model cost INR 13,450 at 0.50.
The binary-feature majority-vote tree ensemble achieved 78.8% holdout accuracy and INR 20,400 cost. The three-round AdaBoost simulation achieved 78.8% accuracy and INR 20,400 cost. These simple ensembles are useful benchmarks, but do not establish superiority over the richer logistic model.
These figures are offline estimates, not guaranteed savings. The dataset is synthetic and small, the holdout has only 51 no-shows, and threshold costs are sensitive to prevalence and the assumed INR cost ratio. Reusing the holdout to select a threshold would bias its reported performance. Binary-only trees also omit five continuous predictors; the neural-network architectures sized in this analysis are design calculations, not trained models or evidence that imaging or referral-note inputs are available.
Before deployment, validate on a larger, time-separated sample; confirm false-alarm and missed-visit costs with operations; calibrate probabilities and choose a threshold using validation data; then run a monitored pilot. Track outreach volume, captured no-shows, staff workload, subgroup performance, and realized costs. Keep scheduling decisions reviewable by staff and refresh the model only after drift and data-quality checks. Use an operations-approved contact cap and escalation policy during the pilot, and compare contacted patients with a suitable control group where feasible. The estimated INR amounts are per 240-record holdout, not annual savings or a forecast of the full patient population.
